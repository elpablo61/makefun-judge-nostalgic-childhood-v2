One sentence, 212 characters, that brings back a childhood memory: a father,
a biscuit tin of old photographs, and a mother at seventeen with a bicycle she
never learned to ride.

`main.ts` (node22) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. The gate needs
UTF-8 text of 1 to 280 characters, a single clean sentence.
